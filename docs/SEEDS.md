# The seed book

## How Seed works

A random fighter is made of three of the cast. Its head, body, arms and legs come from those three, its moves are
dealt from them a move group at a time (its punches one's, its kicks another's, its throws a third's, and so on), its
stance is the first's and its voice one of theirs. Its name is made from theirs, and you see it on the energy bar:
JACPAIWOLF, SAJACWOLF, WOLFLAURAH.

Type that name as a **Seed** and you get that fighter again: the same build and the same moves, on any machine. Only
the colours change: they're new every game.

- **Offline:** Menu → Cheats → **Player 1 fighter** (or **Player 2 fighter**) → **Seed**, then type the name.
- **Online:** Online → Settings → **Your fighter** → **Seed**, then type the name. Your opponent sees it under your
  name on the VS screen. (With a random fighter you meet players who have one too.)

A name that isn't in this book says "No fighter has the seed ...". Every Random fighter you meet has one of these
names (Random and Random opponents draw from the whole book), so if you liked one, write down its name from the
energy bar.

**One recipe per name, across the games.** The first 337 names are Virtua Fighter 1 Recomp's book, carried over: each is
the same three (the first leading), the same head and the same voice as in VF1, and its moves are dealt by the same
rule from its name, so it plays the same mix (a head VF2 doesn't have, a VF1 cut character's, is the first's). The
rest are VF2's own, each with Lion or Shun among its three; VF3 Recomp carries this whole book on the same way.
`docs/random_book.txt` is the book as a table of recipes (name, the three, head, voice: what the next game carries),
made by `vf2random --recipes`; `vf2random --book` makes the table below.

What the columns mean: **Body** is the torso and hips, **Arms** the upper arms, forearms and hands, **Legs** the
thighs, shins and feet. **Moves** lists the three, the stance's owner first, each with its move groups: P, K, P+K,
K+G, P+K+G, throws (P+G), and the rest.
